Evolving NCAs with Epiplexity

What dynamics fall out of a (μ+λ) evolution strategy when we explicitly target a gzip-compression ratio? Below: six runs that share everything except the target compressibility of the rollout.

Repository: shuyi-gin-wang/synthetic-pre-pre-training · branch probe-score-experiments · script scripts/evolve_nca_preq_gzip_continuous_torch.py

Setup

  • 16×16 continuous NCA, 3 state channels, Δt=0.05.
  • Fitness = preq_length × gaussian-band gate around a target gzip ratio: exp(-(gzip - target)2 / (2·0.22)).
  • preq_length is the prequential length: a linear probe is fit on rollout transitions, and we sum the per-step MSE above the final loss floor — high values mean the dynamics are not trivially predictable by the probe.
  • (μ+λ) ES with pop=128, elite=16, σ: 0.1 → ~0.005 over 600 generations.
  • Trajectories below are rolled out from a fresh uniform initial condition with no dropout.

What we found

  1. The gzip gate works as a coarse complexity dial. Best-of-run gzip closely tracks the target up through ~0.85; beyond that the achieved ratio saturates around 0.89 even when the target is 1.0.
  2. Preq length is non-monotonic in the gzip target. The highest preq_length we saw came at target=0.85 (preq ≈ 10.7), not at the chaotic end. Pushing for fully uncompressible noise (target=1.0) doesn't buy more probe-difficulty.
  3. Visual character changes smoothly. Low targets give stable, blocky patterns; mid targets give travelling structures; high targets give shimmering, noise-like fields. Watch the 1000-step extended rollouts to see whether structure persists.
